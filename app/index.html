Three steps.
All you.

  1. Set the scene.

    Drop one or several photos into the studio. They fill the panels automatically. Or start with a sample scene.

  2. Find your angle.

    Click a panel, then a photo to swap it. Drag the photo on the cover to pan, and use Zoom to crop closer. Pick a layout, a mood, and a headline.

  3. Make the feed.

    Choose Square, Portrait, or Wide and hit Export cover for a full-size PNG. The large preview lets you check every detail.

TIP: Use arrow keys on the cover to pan. Ctrl / Cmd + Z undoes an edit.
